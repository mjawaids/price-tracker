# SpendLess — Blog Ideas, SEO Keywords & Content Calendar

## SEO keyword clusters
Group content around these intent clusters. Mix global terms with emerging-market /
Pakistan terms (where the app is tuned and early traction is likely).

**Core / product:**
- grocery price tracker, price comparison app, shopping optimizer, cheapest grocery app,
  compare grocery prices, price tracking app, grocery budgeting app

**Delivery-fee angle (high-differentiation, low competition):**
- avoid delivery fees groceries, free delivery threshold trick, cheapest way to order
  groceries online, is grocery delivery worth it, minimize delivery charges

**Behavior / how-to:**
- how to save money on groceries, weekly grocery budget plan, how to compare store prices,
  grocery list optimizer, multi-store shopping plan

**Local / emerging-market:**
- grocery prices in Pakistan, cheapest grocery store Karachi/Lahore, save on groceries
  inflation, online grocery Pakistan price comparison, kiryana vs supermarket prices

## On-page SEO checklist (per post)
- One primary keyword in title, H1, first 100 words, and meta description.
- Internal link to the landing page with a benefit-led anchor ("build your cheapest cart").
- One worked numeric example (delivery-fee math) — it's the unique, link-worthy asset.
- CTA block mid-article and at the end → https://spendless.ibexoft.com
- OG image per post; reuse Plan-screen hero for product posts.
