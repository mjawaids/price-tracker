# SpendLess — Brand, Voice & Messaging Guardrails

## Brand basics
- **Product name:** SpendLess (one word, capital S and L).
- **Maker:** Ibexoft.
- **URL:** https://spendless.ibexoft.com
- **Category:** Price tracker + shopping optimizer (mobile-first PWA).

## Positioning statement
For budget-conscious households and multi-store shoppers, **SpendLess** is a price tracker
and shopping optimizer that finds the cheapest way to buy your whole cart across your own
stores — including delivery fees — unlike comparison sites that only know big retailers and
ignore delivery.

## Voice & tone
- **Practical & money-smart** — we talk in real savings, not buzzwords.
- **Friendly, a little cheeky** — confident, never salesy or hypey.
- **Clear over clever** — short sentences, concrete numbers, plain words.
- **Respectful of effort** — we acknowledge setup takes a little work and pays off on repeat.

**Say:** cheapest total, delivery included, your stores, your price book, optimized cart,
spend less, real savings.
**Avoid:** "revolutionary," "AI-powered" (it's deterministic optimization, not ML),
"track millions of products," exaggerated savings guarantees, fake urgency.

## Visual identity (from the app)
- **Accent / primary:** magenta `#df3798`
- **Background:** warm cream `#fcfaf6`
- **Theme:** single light theme (no dark mode — by design).
- **Fonts in the app:** Bricolage Grotesque (display/headlines), Hanken Grotesk (UI/body),
  Space Mono (numbers/prices). Mirror these on the marketing site for consistency.
- **Feel:** clean, warm, uncluttered, mobile-first, generous touch targets.

## Boilerplate (reusable "about" blurbs)

**One sentence:**
SpendLess is a mobile-first price tracker and shopping optimizer that finds the cheapest way
to buy your whole cart across your stores — delivery fees included.

**Short (≈40 words):**
SpendLess helps you spend less on the same groceries. Track what each store charges, build
your cart, and get an optimized, delivery-aware shopping plan in one tap. Works in 50+
currencies, installs to your phone like a native app, and keeps your data private.

**Press/about (≈70 words):**
SpendLess (by Ibexoft) is a mobile-first price-comparison app and shopping optimizer for
everyday buying. Instead of relying on a fixed list of big retailers, it lets shoppers track
prices at their own stores — local shops and online grocers alike — then computes the
cheapest way to buy an entire cart, factoring in each store's delivery rules. Supporting 50+
currencies with automatic detection and built as an installable web app, SpendLess is
designed for budget-conscious households, especially in high-inflation markets.

- "Factors delivery fees and free-delivery thresholds into every plan."
- "Shows how much you saved versus buying each item at its lowest price."
- "Supports 50+ currencies with automatic detection."
- "Installable mobile-first app (add to home screen, runs full-screen)."

- "Private by design — per-user data, no ads."
- "Physical and online store types, with four delivery-rule options."
- "15 built-in shopping categories, plus unlimited custom ones."

## 🚫 DO NOT CLAIM (not implemented — do not let any AI invent these)
- ❌ Price-drop alerts / deal notifications / **push notifications** (toggle exists, no backend).
- ❌ **Favorites / wishlist** (heart icon is non-functional).
- ❌ **Multiple shopping lists** (only "My Cart" is active).
- ❌ **Price history / trends / charts over time** (only current prices are stored).
- ❌ **Offline mode / works without internet** (installable, but not offline-first).
- ❌ **Location-based store filtering / "stores near you"** (location is informational only).
- ❌ **Automatic price scraping / live retailer feeds** (prices are user-entered).
- ❌ **Dark mode** (light theme by design).
- ❌ **AI/ML** language (optimization is deterministic brute-force + greedy).
- ❌ Specific savings guarantees (e.g., "save 30%") — use "spend less" / show example math instead.

## Savings claims — how to phrase responsibly
Prefer **mechanism and examples** over guarantees:
- ✅ "Shows your savings versus buying each item at its lowest price."
- ✅ A worked example: "Naive total $52.40 → optimized $46.10. You saved $6.30."
- ❌ "Save up to 40% on groceries!" (unsubstantiated).

## When unsure
If an AI or writer proposes a feature not on the approved list, treat it as false until
verified against the app. The truthful core is strong enough — lead with the optimizer and
the delivery-fee angle.
