# SpendLess — Features → Benefits

Every feature below exists in the shipping app. Each is paired with the user benefit
(use these for feature pages, comparison tables, ad copy, and bullet lists).

## 1. Delivery-aware cart optimizer  ⭐ headline
- **Feature:** Splits your cart across stores to minimize the total cost, factoring in
  each store's delivery rule and your subtotal at that store. Brute-forces the optimal
  plan for normal carts; uses a fast greedy method for very large ones.
- **Benefit:** One tap turns "where do I buy all this for the least money?" into a clear
  answer — and shows how much you saved versus buying each item at its lowest price.
- **Copy hook:** *The cheapest item isn't the cheapest trip. We do the math.*

## 2. Per-store price tracking (your personal price book)
- **Feature:** Record what each store charges for each product. Prices, currency, and
  availability are tracked per store, per item.
- **Benefit:** Stop relying on memory or scattered notes. Your price knowledge lives in
  one place and powers every plan.
- **Copy hook:** *Finally, a place for "it's cheaper at the other store."*

## 3. Smart "Where to buy" on every product
- **Feature:** Each product page lists every store that stocks it, sorted cheapest-first,
  with a BEST badge on the lowest price and each store's delivery label.
- **Benefit:** Instant clarity on the best place to buy any single item.
- **Copy hook:** *See who has it cheapest, at a glance.*

## 4. Delivery rules that match the real world
- **Feature:** Set each store's delivery as none, always free, flat fee, or free-over-a-
  threshold (with the fee below it). Physical and online stores supported.
- **Benefit:** The plan reflects how stores actually charge — including "free over $40."
- **Copy hook:** *Free over $40? We know — and we'll get you over the line if it's cheaper.*

## 5. Build a cart in seconds
- **Feature:** Browse by category or search your catalog, tap + to add, adjust quantities
  with steppers. Cart shows item count, product count, and an estimated subtotal.
- **Benefit:** Going from "I need these things" to an optimized plan takes under a minute.
- **Copy hook:** *Tap, tap, plan.*

## 6. Browse by aisle, search with recents
- **Feature:** A categorized "aisle" view across 15 built-in categories (Fruits &
  Vegetables, Dairy & Eggs, Rice/Atta & Pulses, and more) plus unlimited custom ones.
  Search remembers your recent lookups.
- **Benefit:** Your catalog feels like a familiar store, organized the way you shop.
- **Copy hook:** *Your shop, organized by aisle.*

## 7. 50+ currencies, auto-detected
- **Feature:** Supports 50+ world currencies and detects a sensible default from your
  device; one tap to change, with quick presets for major cities.
- **Benefit:** Works out of the box wherever you are, with prices that look right.
- **Copy hook:** *Built for your wallet, in your currency.*

## 8. Tuned for everyday, real-world shopping
- **Feature:** Categories and defaults are tuned for daily essentials in emerging markets
  (groceries, household, pharmacy, baby, personal care).
- **Benefit:** Especially valuable where prices swing the most and every rupee/dollar counts.
- **Copy hook:** *Made for the weekly shop, not just big-ticket gadgets.*

## 9. Add products with images
- **Feature:** Create products with name, size/unit, category, brand, and an uploaded image.
- **Benefit:** A catalog that's quick to scan and genuinely yours.

## 10. Secure sign-in, private data
- **Feature:** Email/password or Google sign-in. All data is per-user and isolated with
  database row-level security.
- **Benefit:** Your stores, prices, and cart are private to you. No ads, no selling data.
- **Copy hook:** *Your prices are nobody's business but yours.*

## 11. Installable, mobile-first PWA
- **Feature:** Add SpendLess to your home screen and it runs full-screen like a native app.
  Layout adapts from phone (bottom tabs) to tablet to desktop (sidebar).
- **Benefit:** App-like speed and feel with nothing to download from a store.
- **Copy hook:** *Add to home screen. Done.*

## 12. Clean, fast, focused design
- **Feature:** A single warm light theme, large touch targets, and a distraction-free UI.
- **Benefit:** Pleasant to use on a quick shop; no clutter between you and the answer.
