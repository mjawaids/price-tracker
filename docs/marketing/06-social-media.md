**The delivery trap (thread starter):**
> Buying every grocery item at its lowest price is often NOT the cheapest way to shop.
> Why? Delivery fees. 🧵

> Item A is cheapest at Store 1. Item B is cheapest at Store 2. Item C at Store 3.
> Buy each at its lowest price → you just paid 3 delivery fees. 💸

> The cheapest *trip* might be buying all three at Store 2 — one delivery, slightly higher
> item prices, lower total.
> Nobody does this math by hand. SpendLess does it in one tap. 👉 spendless.ibexoft.com

**Free-over-threshold hook:**
> "Free delivery over $40."
> So… do you add $6 of stuff to save the $4 fee? Sometimes yes, sometimes no.
> SpendLess works out the cheaper option for your *whole* cart automatically. 🧮

**Feature — currencies:**

**Feature — your stores:**

> Your prices. Your plan. 🏷️

**One-liner posts (rotate):**
