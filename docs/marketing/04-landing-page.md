## Problem (3-up)
**Section title:** Buying everything at its lowest price isn't actually the cheapest.
1. **Prices vary store to store.** Nobody can track it all in their head.
2. **Delivery fees hide the real cost.** Flat fees and "free over $X" change the math.
3. **The cheapest split is a hard puzzle.** With a full cart across several stores there
   are millions of combinations.

---

## Solution (how it works, 4 steps)
**Section title:** Your personal price book that does the math.
1. **Add your stores** — local or online, with their delivery rules.
2. **Record prices** — what each store charges for each item.
3. **Build your cart** — browse or search, tap to add.
4. **Get your cheapest plan** — an optimized split across stores, delivery included.

*(Pair each step with a screenshot: Manage Stores → Manage Prices → Cart → Plan.)*

## FAQ
**Is SpendLess free?** Yes — start using it free at spendless.ibexoft.com.
**Do I have to install an app?** No. It runs in your browser; optionally add it to your
home screen to use it like a native app.
**Which stores does it support?** Any store you choose — local shops and online grocers
alike. You add the stores and prices that matter to you.
**How does it find the cheapest plan?** It checks how to split your cart across stores and
picks the combination with the lowest total cost, including each store's delivery fee.
**Does it work in my currency?** It supports 50+ currencies and auto-detects a sensible
default; you can change it anytime.
**Is my data private?** Yes. Your data is tied to your account and isolated per-user. No ads.
**Can I sign in with Google?** Yes — email/password or Google.
