# SpendLess — Screenshot Shot-List

These are the screenshots to capture for the landing page, blog, app directories, and
social. Capture against the live app (https://spendless.ibexoft.com) with a **realistic,
populated demo account** — the optimizer only looks impressive with real data.

> Note: I couldn't auto-generate these in the build environment because the app is
> auth-gated and no demo data/keys are present here. If you share access (or a seeded demo
> login), I can capture and crop these for you.

## Prep: seed a believable demo account
- **5–8 stores:** mix of physical and online; vary delivery rules — at least one
  "always free," one "flat fee," one "free over $X," one "no delivery."
- **20–30 products** across several categories, each with images and prices at 2–4 stores
  so the optimizer has real choices to make.
- **A cart of ~6–10 products** with a couple of quantities >1, designed so the optimal plan
  actually splits across 2+ stores and shows a non-trivial "you saved" number.
- Pick a currency that reads well for your primary audience (PKR for local, USD for global).

## Capture settings
- **Device frames:** iPhone-size (≈390×844) for the hero/social; also a desktop (≈1440 wide)
  shot for the sidebar layout. The app is responsive — grab both.
- **Hero export:** 1200×630 for OG/social cards.
- Clean status bar; consistent currency; no half-empty states (except the intentional
  "empty cart" shot).

## Priority order
If you only capture a few: **#5 (Plan)** first — it's the hero and the differentiator —
then **#3 (Detail / where to buy)**, **#4 (Cart)**, and **#6 (Manage Stores delivery rules)**.

## Annotated versions (for the feature spotlight)
Create one annotated version of **#5 (Plan)** with callouts pointing to:
- the grand total,
- a store's delivery line (esp. a "Free" one earned by crossing a threshold),
- the "Split across N stores for the lowest total — you saved $Y" line.
This single annotated image is your most persuasive marketing asset.

## A note on honesty in screenshots
Don't stage features that don't exist (price-drop alerts, favorites, multiple lists, price
history). Capture only real screens with real data. See `08-brand-and-messaging.md`.
